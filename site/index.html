Every animation runs on one of four clocks

Name the clock first

It decides the cost, the engine and which traps apply. Trigger first; promote to scroll or media only where the reader should drive the motion.

Clock 1 of 4

Trigger

Plays once when content arrives: an entrance on the measured curve, a headline split into words by the build, a number counting up. reveal, split-words, split-reveal, count-up.

Clock 2 of 4

Scroll

A function of the reader's position in a range: this pinned scene, the rail below, the header's ink. Under ScrollSmoother every scroll-linked value comes from ScrollTrigger. pinned-scene, horizontal-rail, header-theme.

Clock 3 of 4

Media

Follows what a video decoder presents: a scrubbed video, an image sequence on a canvas, a loop with a pause control. Heavy media is prepared at build time and probed, never assumed. scrub-video, frame-sequence, loop-video.

Clock 4 of 4

Render

A render loop: WebGL or a canvas drawing every frame, on the same tick as the scroll. Per-frame work stops off-screen, in hidden tabs and in hidden routes.

Each job goes to the engine that does it best

CSS for decoration, Motion for React component and section motion, GSAP for timelines and its plugins. When a route already loads GSAP, its entrances use GSAP rather than adding Motion.

The blocks on this page

Copied into src/animation/ by scroll-animation add and recorded in its lock file. The page only wires them.

  • smooth-smoother ScrollSmoother owns the scroll; reduced motion runs it in native mode
  • pinned-scene the four clocks, pinned by ScrollTrigger
  • horizontal-rail the engine map; a native snap scroller under reduced motion
  • split-words the headline, split into words when the site is built
  • split-reveal these headings, line by line once their font is in
  • reveal the copy, on the measured entrance curve
  • count-up the numbers above, from the final value in the HTML
  • header-theme the header's ink, from the section under it
  • mount creates them in page order on the route's root

One scroll authority per page

This page runs ScrollSmoother, so sticky, fixed and CSS scroll timelines are out inside its content: the pins are ScrollTrigger's, and the header lives outside the wrapper. The guide scrolls natively and runs its decoration on CSS timelines.
